# History Unfolded v1.8

Balance and historical legibility update.

- Dynasties persist longer and expose realms governed, complete ruler lists, peak population, peak GDP, peak territory, wars won and Wonders.
- Merchant, trade-route and commercial-building effects create stronger GDP divergence.
- Evolution adds Military Power based on non-civil wars won/lost, battles won/lost, casualty balance and current army size.
- Wars can end in stalemate and all realm/war views clearly identify victory, defeat, active war or stalemate.
- Culture strength now creates cross-border political pressure when a stronger neighboring culture has a different government or religion.
- Save schema: history-unfolded-saves-v18.
